Proposed 6-week pilot · Requires county agreement

Make every referral visible and owned.

  • Owner: county referral lead and one specialist reviewer.
  • Week 1: agree the cohort, target times and baseline; inspect incomplete referrals.
  • Weeks 2–5: test a common referral checklist, named handoff owner and daily review of unacknowledged referrals in two clinics.
  • Week 6: compare acknowledgment time and treatment-within-target share; check reviewer workload and digital access.

Suggested workflow, not a clinically validated intervention. Staffing and escalation thresholds must be agreed locally.

Demo data · Not findings about Finnish counties

All percentages, waits, comparisons and scenarios on this page are invented design examples. Sample counties A–C are fictional. No live dataset or AI service is connected. General specialist waiting lists must not be presented as cancer-specific delays.

Before a county pilot

Confirm the cancer cohort, target definition, reporting period, denominator and missing-data rate with the county. Validate the proposed intervention with clinicians and mentors. Publish comparable baseline and follow-up measures; do not infer causality from a regional difference.
