## Form limits (ceilings, not targets)
Name 30 characters · punchline 200 · description 5,000 (markdown) · presentation PDF up to 20 MB.

## What this means for us
- 55% of the score (Actionability + Insight) is about a concrete, located gap and what to do about it. Our prototype shows a service, not a gap analysis.
- Two minimum requirements are **not met today**: no Goal 3 indicator is cited, and no public data source backs a claim. See REPORT.md.
- "Execution" (15%) needs a live demo. The demo runs locally; there is no public link yet.
